Portuguese morphology reference: **mlconjug3**, Ars-Linguistica and contributors,
MIT, commit `691b199c60c523edcc3f0753390f02fbd8714e0b`.
https://github.com/Ars-Linguistica/mlconjug3/tree/691b199c60c523edcc3f0753390f02fbd8714e0b/mlconjug3/data/conjug_manager
Its license is preserved in `licenses/mlconjug3-MIT.txt`. Source conjugation
tables and machine-learning models are not included in the application.

Project modifications: selected 5000 Brazilian corpus tokens in frequency order;
filtered separate proper names, foreign noise, fragments and obsolete spelling;
retained and reviewed Portuguese grammatical contractions and common abbreviations;
reviewed all Russian translations and Brazilian meanings; added common senses,
sense-bound synonyms, topic assignments and conjugation-family exclusions;
authored 500 expression and 500 verb-construction study pairs with Russian
translations and ambiguity-exclusion families.

Vocabulary, translations and study collections: **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
Application code: MIT. These study collections are not a statistical ranking
of the 500 most frequent expressions or verb constructions.
